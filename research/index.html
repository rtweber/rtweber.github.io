CS Education Research

My CS Education work focuses on practical ways to teach theory courses more effectively. Usually these projects come from seeing a problem in one of my classes, coming up with an idea for improving it (or getting an idea from a TA), and then evaluating whether the interverntion made a difference.

CS Theory Research

During my Ph.D. program, my research focused on combinatorial approaches to questions relevant to (theoretical) computer scientists. I worked mainly on matching problems.

These days, I am not active in cutting edge theory research, though I do occasionally advise mid-career undergraduates in theory research.
